Frequently asked questions

When can I start?

Whenever you want: the sample lessons are available now and you go at your own pace.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of completion when you finish every lesson. It is not an official professional accreditation.

How do I pay?

With PayPal, which accepts cards. For now payments are in preview and nothing is charged.

Are there discounts for groups or institutions?

Yes. Schools, camps and NGOs can request institutional training from the pricing page.
